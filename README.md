AI-Powered Portfolio Risk Intelligence at BlackRockAn academic case study exploring how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML) can enhance risk identification, prioritization, and decision support within BlackRock's Aladdin investment platform.  📌 Case Study OverviewBusiness Problem: Identifying and prioritizing emerging portfolio risks across high-volume, multi-asset global investments faster than manual analysis allows.  Core Proposal: Implement an AI/ML decision-support layer to detect hidden anomalies, run stress scenarios, and provide natural-language risk explanations without replacing human portfolio managers.  Context & Platform: BBA FinTech project (Chitkara Business School) analyzing technology-driven risk management in asset management.  🏗️ System Architecture & WorkflowData Ingestion: Aggregates portfolio holdings, market data, risk factors, macroeconomic indicators, and unstructured textual data (e.g., filings, central bank communications).  ML Risk Analytics: Utilizes machine learning for anomaly detection, predictive risk pattern identification, and factor correlation.  Generative AI Layer: Uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to convert complex analytics into actionable, plain-language risk summaries and handle natural-language queries.  Human-in-the-Loop: Risk professionals validate all model-generated alerts before executing portfolio adjustments.  ⚖️ Governance & Responsible AI FrameworkData Privacy: Role-based access controls, encryption, and strict audit trails for sensitive client data.  Model Explainability: Feature importance and explainable AI (XAI) outputs to avoid black-box decision-making.  Risk Mitigations: Strict validation checks against generative AI hallucinations, continuous monitoring for model drift/bias, and explicit human oversight protocols.  👥 Roster & ContributionsDeveloped by BBA FinTech students at Chitkara Business School.
